Cozy Lina

About Cozy Lina

Cozy Lina is a small reading space for people with a busy mind. We publish short, honest stories and articles about overthinking, sleep and gentle everyday habits, the kind of thoughts that get loud at 2 AM and sound much softer in the morning.

Who Lina is

Lina is a fictional teenager who overthinks, apologizes too much, and checks her phone more than she would like. Her best friend Zoe is practical, funny, and always has a pencil in her curls. Every story follows one small worry and one small, doable tool: a two-column notebook page, a sticky note that says “Roll credits”, a jam jar for sorrys.

What you will find here

  • Stories you can read in about ten minutes, in plain, warm language.
  • Gentle tools you can try tonight, like writing a worry on paper or asking a thought for its ID.
  • Printable versions of the stories with journal pages, for people who like to write by hand.

How we write

Every story is reviewed and edited before it is published, and the illustrations are created for Cozy Lina. We aim to be honest: we do not promise cures, and we never use fear to get your attention.

A note on health

Our content is for comfort and information. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional care. If anxiety or sleeplessness affects your daily life, please talk to a doctor or a licensed therapist. You can find more support on our Need help? page.
